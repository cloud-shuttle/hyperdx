## Certification Program

### ClickStack Certified Professional (CCP)

#### Certification Overview
The ClickStack Certified Professional (CCP) certification validates expertise in ClickStack implementation, configuration, and management.

#### Certification Requirements
- Complete all training modules
- Pass comprehensive exam
- Submit practical project
- Maintain continuing education

#### Exam Structure
- **Duration**: 3 hours
- **Format**: Multiple choice and practical
- **Passing Score**: 80%
- **Topics**: All training modules

#### Practical Project
- Implement ClickStack in test environment
- Configure all major features
- Generate comprehensive report
- Present findings and recommendations

### ClickStack Certified Administrator (CCA)

#### Certification Overview
The ClickStack Certified Administrator (CCA) certification validates advanced skills in ClickStack administration, optimization, and troubleshooting.

#### Certification Requirements
- Hold CCP certification
- Complete advanced modules
- Pass advanced exam
- Submit advanced project
- Demonstrate practical experience

#### Advanced Exam Structure
- **Duration**: 4 hours
- **Format**: Advanced scenarios and troubleshooting
- **Passing Score**: 85%
- **Topics**: Advanced configuration and optimization

#### Advanced Project
- Design and implement enterprise ClickStack solution
- Optimize for high-scale deployment
- Implement security and compliance measures
- Create operational procedures

### Certification Maintenance

#### Continuing Education Requirements
- **Annual Requirements**: 20 hours of continuing education
- **Topics**: New features, best practices, industry trends
- **Format**: Online courses, webinars, conferences

#### Recertification
- **Frequency**: Every 2 years
- **Requirements**: Pass updated exam
- **Options**: Full recertification or continuing education
